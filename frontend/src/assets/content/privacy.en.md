This site only collects the data needed to reply to your message. It uses no cookies for analytics or advertising.

## Data controller

The data controller is Michał Pośpieszyński. For anything about your personal data, write to [pospieszynski.michal@gmail.com](mailto:pospieszynski.michal@gmail.com).

## What data is collected

- Contact form data: your name, email address, company (if you give one), topic, message and – for project enquiries – the chosen service, budget and timeline.
- A hash of your IP address (one-way, with a secret salt), used only to protect the form from spam and too many messages. The IP address itself is not stored.
- The site uses no tracking cookies. Visits are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics without cookies and without identifying anyone.

## Purpose and legal basis

- Replying to your message – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given in the form.
- Talks before a possible contract – steps taken at your request before entering into it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Protecting the form from abuse – the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Retention

Contact messages are deleted automatically after 12 months. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

## Processors

Your data is processed on my behalf by these service providers, under data processing agreements:

- **Supabase** – database, administrator sign-in and file storage; servers in the European Union.
- **Cloudflare** – delivering the site, security, bot protection (Turnstile) and cookieless analytics.
- **Email provider (SMTP)** – sending the new-message notification and the automatic reply.
- **Server provider (VPS)** – the server the application runs on; in the European Union.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO).

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies for analytics or advertising. It keeps only two things in your browser's storage (`localStorage`): the theme you chose (light or dark) and that you closed the English-version hint. On the contact page, Cloudflare Turnstile may store what it needs for bot protection.

## Changes

This policy may change as the site evolves. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
