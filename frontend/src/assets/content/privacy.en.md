This site collects the data needed to reply to your message and, if you agree, data on how the site is used. It uses no cookies for advertising.

## Data controller

The data controller is Michał Pośpieszyński. For anything about your personal data, write to [pospieszynski.michal@gmail.com](mailto:pospieszynski.michal@gmail.com).

## What data is collected

- Contact form data: your name, email address, company (if you give one), topic, message and – for project enquiries – the chosen service, budget and timeline.
- A hash of your IP address (one-way, with a secret salt), used only to protect the form from spam and too many messages. The IP address itself is not stored.
- Visits are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics without cookies and without identifying anyone.
- Site usage statistics (PostHog), depending on your choice in the cookie banner:
  - **If you accept:** pages viewed, clicks (including where on the page – heatmaps), scrolling and recordings of the visit, linked by an anonymous identifier stored in a cookie. Recordings mask everything typed into forms; no name, email address or message text is recorded. You are never identified by name.
  - **If you reject:** only anonymous page views and clicks on key buttons, without cookies. PostHog counts them with a hash calculated on its servers and does not link them across days.
  - **Before you choose:** nothing.
  - The admin pages are never tracked.

## Purpose and legal basis

- Replying to your message – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given in the form.
- Talks before a possible contract – steps taken at your request before entering into it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Protecting the form from abuse – the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Usage statistics with cookies, heatmaps and recordings – your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given in the cookie banner.
- Anonymous page counts without cookies – the controller's legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Retention

Contact messages are deleted automatically after 12 months. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time. PostHog keeps visit recordings for up to 30 days and usage statistics for up to 12 months.

Your data is processed on my behalf by these service providers, under data processing agreements:

- **Cloudflare** – delivering the site, security, bot protection (Turnstile) and cookieless analytics.
- **PostHog** (PostHog Inc.) – usage statistics, heatmaps and visit recordings; data stored in the European Union (Frankfurt). PostHog Inc. is a US company; transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Email provider (SMTP)** – sending the new-message notification and the automatic reply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO).

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies for advertising. In your browser's storage (`localStorage`) it keeps the theme you chose (light or dark), the language you chose for the site, that you closed the English-version hint, and your answer to the cookie banner (with its date; you are asked again after 12 months). On the contact page, Cloudflare Turnstile may store what it needs for bot protection.

Only if you accept in the cookie banner, PostHog stores an anonymous identifier in a cookie and in `localStorage` (`ph_…_posthog`, kept for up to a year), so that visits can be linked.

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing removes PostHog's cookie and stored data from your browser. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, the site treats it as a rejection.
